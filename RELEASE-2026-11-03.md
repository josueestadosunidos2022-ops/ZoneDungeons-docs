## English
New info, pending loot and diagnostic commands, draft cleanup tools and delayed replacement of unloaded room mobs. Physical dungeons and farming spots retain saved data. Requires ZoneLevelUp beta29-r50 or newer; use the same Dungeons version on both sides.
